About me

Ignacio Vidal Domínguez

Before human-centered leadership became fashionable, I was already the executive people came to when they had a problem they couldn't take to anyone else. Not because I read a book on organizational culture, but because I always believed the only real way to grow is by genuinely trusting people.
I put it into practice at every company I worked for, and the results were always the same: teams that stopped waiting for instructions and started bringing solutions.
That same approach is what held up the numbers. Leading the full P&L of Volvo Chile (14 dealerships, 250 employees), I defined and executed the expansion strategy: I approved a USD 30 million investment to double the network from 7 to 14 points, and market share went from around 8% to nearly 20%. None of those numbers came out of a spreadsheet: they came from teams that stopped waiting for instructions.
Over time I found in executive coaching the methodology that put a name to what I had always done intuitively. I became a Certified Executive Coach through ICF and completed a postgraduate degree in Organizational Balance Management. Not to change the way I work, but to deepen it.
Today I offer what the market didn't have: coming into an organization as a Fractional COO with executive coaching training, and working simultaneously on leadership, operations and full digitalization. The promise is simple: when we're done, the team can stand on its own.
